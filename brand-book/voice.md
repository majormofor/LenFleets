# Voice and copy

LensFleet writes like a calm, competent colleague who has already looked at the footage. We state what happened, where and when; we reassure by being specific, not by being cheerful. The same voice serves a bus operator in Lagos, a care manager in Croydon and a vlogger on a motorbike — only the vocabulary shifts.

## Principles

1. **Fact first, feeling second.** "Bus 14 arrived at Oshodi at 07:42. Clip saved." Then, if needed, "Everything is on schedule."
2. **Specific beats soft.** Times, places and counts are the product. Never round them away in copy.
3. **Short sentences. Sentence case.** Headings, buttons, labels and notifications are sentence case. No exclamation marks anywhere in the product.
4. **You, your, and only sometimes we.** Address the reader. "We" is LensFleet the company — in legal copy, release notes and the website's about page — not the app talking about itself.
5. **Say what it is.** A clip is a clip, a position is a position. Avoid metaphors in interface copy; keep them for the brand story.
6. **No hype words.** Not *revolutionary*, *seamless*, *cutting-edge*, *AI-powered* as an adjective. If AI produced a summary, say "Summary" and show the facts it rests on.

## Words we use and words we avoid

| Use | Avoid | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| verified, recorded, proof, evidence | surveillance, spy, snoop | We sell confidence, not suspicion. |
| see, watch, view (people) · track, locate (vehicles, assets, pets) | track or monitor (people) | People are seen; things are tracked. |
| live, live view | streaming, feed | Plain words; "feed" is also an animal word in the pet sector. |
| recording, clip | footage, bodycam footage | "Footage" sounds like a news report about someone else. |
| share, share link | broadcast, publish (except for creators) | Sharing is deliberate and revocable. |
| alert | alarm, warning (except severity labels) | Alerts inform; alarms frighten. |
| space, members | account, tenant, organisation (in-app) | The product's own structure, in words a household understands. |
| Can manage · Can edit · Can view | admin, owner, editor, viewer, roles | The three permission levels, always written exactly so. |
| older relative, parent, client | the elderly, patient, subject | Dignity. |
| it's fine to say "we don't know yet" | fake certainty | A position with no fix is "last seen 12 min ago", never a guessed dot. |

## The copy dictionary

One interface, two vocabularies. The build flavour sets the words; the meaning is the same.

| Concept | Business (Sentinel) | Household (LensFleet Home) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The thing being looked after | Fleet · Vehicle · Asset | Home · Car · Family member · Pet |
| The person doing a job | Driver · Operator · Guard · Carer | (not used — members are family) |
| The group of people | Team · Members | Family · Members |
| The container | Space | Space (shown as "household") |
| Default permission on invite | Can view | Can edit |
| Sign-up question | "Who are you keeping an eye on?" → Business | → Household |
| A proof | Verified delivery · Visit verified · Incident recording | Home safe · Arrived · Clip saved |

Sector words sit inside the Business column: a security company sees *Guard* and *Incident*, a care agency *Carer* and *Visit*, a courier *Driver* and *Delivery*. They are labels, not features.

## Examples

Notification, delivery sector
> Bus 14 arrived at Oshodi at 07:42. 23 passengers. Clip saved.

Notification, care sector
> Grace arrived at Mrs Adeyemi's at 09:58 and left at 10:46. Visit verified.

Notification, household
> Rex left the garden at 16:04. Last seen on Adewale Street.

Empty state, assets
> No assets yet. Add a tracker to see where things are.

Error
> We couldn't reach the camera. It was last online 12 minutes ago. Check its power and try again.

Share dialog
> This link shows the journey and the clip for 24 hours. Anyone with the link can view; nobody can edit or download.

Marketing headline
> Proof in motion.

Marketing support line
> Cameras and trackers that turn "it was delivered", "the carer came" and "the guard was there" into something you can see.

## Formatting

- Dates: 12 Oct 2026 (day month year, no ordinal). Times: 24-hour, 07:42. Durations: 48 min, 2 h 10 min.
- Units: km and km/h by default; mi and mph where the space's region is the UK and the user has chosen imperial.
- Currency: £ and ₦ with the symbol, no decimals for whole amounts.
- Numbers in copy: words up to nine, digits from 10, always digits for counts in the product.
- Capitalisation: product names as written (Sentinel, LensFleet Home); features in lower case (live view, journey replay, geofence) unless starting a sentence.
