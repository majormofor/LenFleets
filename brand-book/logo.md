# Logo

## Concept

The LensFleet mark is a falcon drawn inside a circle. The falcon stands for speed, precision and elevated sight — seeing clearly from above and moving with purpose. The circle is the lens and the sense of continuous motion. Within the curves of the head and wing sit the letters "L" (the sweep of the wing) and "F" (the beak and brow), so the mark carries the name without spelling it. Minimal detail and a single ink keep it legible at a favicon and dignified on the side of a bus.

## The files

All logo files live in the **Logos** asset group. They are the only versions of the mark; do not redraw, trace or recolour them.

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `lensfleet-lockup-graphite.svg` | Primary logo on light grounds (`surface`, `surface-raised`, `graphite-300` tints). Websites, documents, decks, letterhead. |
| `lensfleet-lockup-white.svg` | Primary logo on dark grounds (`graphite-900`, photography, video). |
| `lensfleet-mark-graphite.svg` | Mark alone on light grounds. App icons, favicons, avatars, navigation, vehicle livery. |
| `lensfleet-mark-white.svg` | Mark alone on dark grounds and on a solid tint of `blue`, `red`, `turquoise` or `green` for an app-icon background. |

The wordmark is set in Geom Semibold, "LensFleet" as one word. It is part of the lockup file and is never typed in; if a context cannot carry the file, use the mark alone and set the name in the running typeface.

## Lockup

- Horizontal only: mark to the left of the wordmark, baseline-aligned, the gap fixed in the file.
- Use the lockup wherever the brand is being introduced: website header, decks, documents, email signatures, press.
- Preferred on the brand's own grounds; on photography only where the area behind it is calm and the contrast is at least 4.5:1.

## Mark alone

Use the mark alone where space is limited or the brand is already established:

- App icons (Sentinel and LensFleet Home both use the mark; the product name sits beneath the icon in the OS, never inside it)
- Favicons and browser tabs
- Social media profile images
- Dashboard navigation and the loading state
- Vehicle and body-camera livery, where the name appears elsewhere on the vehicle or uniform

The mark is never altered, rotated, mirrored or combined with another symbol.

## Clear space

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the capital "L" in the wordmark (call it X). No text, graphics, photography edges or other logos enter this zone, on all four sides. For the mark alone, X is one quarter of the mark's diameter.

## Minimum sizes

- Lockup: 120px wide on screen, 30mm wide in print.
- Mark alone: 24px on screen (the favicon is a special export at 16px and 32px, from the same file), 10mm in print.

Below these sizes the wing detail closes up; use the mark alone before the lockup, and plain type before an illegible mark.

## Colour

The mark is graphite (`graphite-900`) or white. Those are the only two inks. For app icons and avatars the mark may sit on a solid field of `blue`, `red`, `turquoise` or `green` at full value, in white — this is the one sanctioned use of colour with the mark and is how the two apps are told apart on a home screen (Sentinel: `graphite-900` field, white mark; LensFleet Home: `blue` field, white mark). Never recolour the mark itself, never use a gradient, never place it on a busy photograph without a solid field behind it.

## Prohibited

- Do not stretch, squash or otherwise distort.
- Do not rotate or tilt.
- Do not change the colours outside the approved palette.
- Do not rearrange the elements or stack the wordmark beneath the mark.
- Do not add shadows, glows, strokes or outlines.
- Do not place the logo inside a shape, badge or container that is not a plain solid field.
- Do not combine with a sector name or another company's mark to make a co-brand lockup; partner logos sit beside ours, separated by a `line` rule and clear space X.

## Naming the products

Sentinel and LensFleet Home have no logos of their own. In product, the mark appears in the navigation and the product name appears as text in `label` style beside it. On marketing pages the lockup carries the brand and the product name is a headline.
