# Sectors

LensFleet is one platform with many fronts. Each sector below shares the same devices, data model, permission levels and visual identity; what changes is the vocabulary in the interface, the default alerts, and the proof moment we design around. Sectors are descriptions, not sub-brands: no sector gets its own colour, logo or wordmark.

For each sector this page gives the customer, the question they need answered, the proof moment, the hardware and product line, the words to use and the imagery to shoot.

## Logistics and delivery

**Who.** Bus and minibus operators, parcel and food couriers, haulage and distribution fleets, water and gas delivery, ride-hailing fleet owners. The pilot customer is a Lagos bus operator.

**The question.** "Did it get there, on the route we agreed, and can I show the customer?"

**Proof moment.** A delivery or a stop with a timestamped clip, the GPS trace against the planned route, and a cabin count where passengers are carried — shareable with the customer by a link that expires.

**What they use.** Sentinel. Vehicle tracker plus forward dashcam; cabin camera for passenger counting on buses; route adherence; proof-of-delivery clips; shareable journey replay.

**Say.** *Verified delivery. Route adherence. Journey replay. Share with the customer.* Not "fleet surveillance", not "driver monitoring".

**Show.** A danfo in traffic from the dashcam's point of view; a courier at a gate with the parcel; the depot at dawn with trackers being fitted.

## Security and law enforcement

**Who.** Private security companies, estate and facilities guards, event security, community policing initiatives and law-enforcement units that need body-worn evidence.

**The question.** "What happened at the incident, who was there, and will the recording stand up?"

**Proof moment.** A body-camera recording that starts at the incident, is live-viewable from the control room, is stored with a tamper-evident access log, and leaves the platform only as a watermarked, revocable export.

**What they use.** Sentinel with body-worn cameras (Shelleyes S7+ class) and docking stations, patrol-vehicle trackers and dashcams, live view for the control room, incident clips, access logging on every view and export.

**Say.** *Evidence. Incident recording. Live view. Chain of custody. Access log.* Not "bodycam footage" in customer-facing copy (say *recording*), not "spy".

**Show.** The camera on the guard's chest, not the guard's face; a control-room screen in a scene; the dock with cameras charging after a shift.

## Health care and elderly care

**Who.** Domiciliary care agencies, home-nursing providers, community health outreach, and families looking after an older relative — the last of these through LensFleet Home.

**The question.** For the agency: "Did the carer attend, when, and for how long?" For the family: "Is Mum all right today, and did the carer come?"

**Proof moment.** A visit verified by the carer's arrival and departure at the client's location with an optional recording of the handover, visible to the agency and — if the client or family chooses — to the family through a share link. For a household: a relative's wearable or phone showing they are where they usually are, with an alert when something changes.

**What they use.** Sentinel for providers (carer body cameras with consent, visit verification, route planning for a round); LensFleet Home for families (a tracker or companion phone for a relative, home cameras, alerts to the family group).

**Say.** *Visit verified. Arrived, departed. Looking after. Peace of mind.* Never "monitoring" of a person; never "elderly" as a noun — say *older relative*, *parent*, *client*. Consent is part of the copy: recordings in care settings are always described as agreed with the person.

**Show.** A carer at a front door, bag in hand; a kettle and two cups; a daughter checking her phone on a bus. Warmth and dignity; no hospital corridors.

## Pet and veterinary care

**Who.** Pet owners (LensFleet Home), and businesses that move or care for animals — mobile vets, dog-walkers and boarders, animal transport, breeders and rescue charities (Sentinel).

**The question.** "Where is the dog?" and, for the business, "Can I show the owner their pet was walked, collected and delivered safely?"

**Proof moment.** A live position on the map and a geofence alert when a pet leaves the garden; for a business, a walk or transport journey with a clip the owner can see.

**What they use.** LensFleet Home with a pet tracker collar (TIZE / ReachFar class devices); Sentinel for the vet van or walker's vehicle with tracker and dashcam, and shareable journey proof for owners.

**Say.** *Where they are. Home safe. Walk verified.* Light and warm, but still precise — "left the garden at 16:04", not "uh-oh!".

**Show.** The collar on the dog at the gate, the map on the owner's phone, the van door opening at the clinic.

## Vlogging and creators

**Who.** Travel and motoring vloggers, cycling and motorbike creators, street and event streamers, and brands running ride-along content.

**The question.** "Can my audience ride along, live, and see where I am while they watch?"

**Proof moment.** A live stream from a body or dash camera with the GPS route alongside it, embedded in the creator's own site or platform, and a journey replay video to post afterwards.

**What they use.** Sentinel's media plane: body or dash camera live over WebRTC, embed tokens for third-party pages, public share links that expire, and server-rendered journey replay (MP4) with the route drawn.

**Say.** *Go live. Ride along. Embed anywhere. Replay the journey.* Energetic but never gimmicky; the product is the lens, the creator is the voice.

**Show.** The camera clipped to a jacket on a bike, the viewer's screen with map and video side by side, the finished replay on a phone.

## Asset management

**Who.** Plant and tool hire, construction firms, generator and equipment owners, container and trailer operators, agricultural equipment owners, and anyone with valuable things that move or should not.

**The question.** "Where is it, is it where it should be, and when did it last move?"

**Proof moment.** Every asset as one record grouping its tracker and any camera, with position, last-moved time, geofence alerts and a history that can be exported for an insurer or a client.

**What they use.** Sentinel with battery and wired trackers, site cameras where present, geofences, daily summaries and exports.

**Say.** *Where it is. Last moved. Left the site. Inventory you can see.* Not "theft prevention" as a headline — we provide knowledge; the customer decides what to do with it.

**Show.** A generator on a site at dusk with a tracker visible, a yard of trailers from above, a tablet in a site office.

## Others

The platform fits any situation in which a person, a vehicle or an object moves and someone needs to know, prove or share what happened. Sectors we expect next, and the copy stance for each:

- **Field service and maintenance** — engineers' visits verified like care visits; say *visit verified*, *on site*.
- **Schools and school transport** — pupil-count and route adherence on school buses; the audience is parents, so lead with *arrived safely* and consent.
- **Events and venues** — temporary security teams and shuttles; everything is time-boxed, so lead with *for the duration of the event*.
- **Agriculture** — equipment, livestock trackers and farm vehicles; the asset story with an outdoor palette of imagery.
- **Emergency and humanitarian response** — vehicle and team location with evidence of delivery of aid; dignity and consent rules as for care.

When a new sector arrives, add a section here in the same shape — who, the question, the proof moment, what they use, say, show — and nothing else changes.
